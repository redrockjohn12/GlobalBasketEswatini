QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

What is the Global Basket runner fee?

Our runner fee is 25% of the product price.

Which stores can I shop from?

Global Basket currently supports SHEIN, AliExpress, Alibaba, Amazon, Banggood, Mr Price, Takealot and Makro.

Do AliExpress and Alibaba have additional charges?

AliExpress and Alibaba may have shipping charges. These are added separately to the product price and runner fee.

Can I pay in South African Rand?

We use South African Rand (ZAR/R) for our displayed estimates to make pricing easier for customers in Eswatini.

How do I place an order?

Find your product, copy its link, then send it to us through WhatsApp. We'll confirm the final price and ordering details.
